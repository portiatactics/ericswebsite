Cornbread

Serves 8 · 35 minutes · 9-inch cast-iron skillet or square pan

Ingredients

  • 1 cup yellow cornmeal
  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1 tbsp baking powder
  • ½ tsp baking soda
  • ¾ tsp salt
  • 2–3 tbsp sugar or honey (optional)
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1¼ cups buttermilk
  • 6 tbsp unsalted butter, melted, plus 1 tbsp for the pan

Method

  1. Put the skillet in the oven and heat both to 425°F (220°C).
  2. Whisk the cornmeal, flour, baking powder, baking soda, salt and sugar in a large bowl.
  3. In another bowl, whisk the eggs, buttermilk and melted butter (and honey, if using).
  4. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry and stir just until combined. A few lumps are fine.
  5. Carefully take out the hot skillet, add 1 tbsp butter and swirl to coat. Pour in the batter. It should sizzle.
  6. Bake 18–22 minutes, until golden and a toothpick in the center comes out clean.
  7. Rest 5 minutes, then slice and serve warm.
